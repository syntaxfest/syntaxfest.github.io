SyntaxFest 2027
Prague, August 30 – September 3

5 events for 1 Syntax Fest

SyntaxFest is a biennial event that brings together a series of events focusing on topics such as empirical syntax, linguistic annotation, statistical language analysis, and natural language processing.

SyntaxFest 2027 will host five events under a unified submission process and program:

  • DepLing: 9th International Conference on Dependency Linguistics
  • IWPT: 19th International Conference on Parsing Technologies
  • QUASY: 4th Workshop on Quantitative Syntax
  • SLiDE: 2nd Workshop on Structured Linguistic Data and Evaluation
  • UDW: 10th Universal Dependencies Workshop
